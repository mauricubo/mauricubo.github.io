**DeutschWeg** takes you from A2 to C2 through 5 levels × 6 units, each with grammar, vocabulary,
mixed practice, reading, dictation and shadowing, writing with a live checklist, and a unit test
that unlocks the next one.

## How it works

- **Spaced repetition**: an SM-2-style scheduler, where cards get harder as they mature
  (recognise → produce → listen).
- **Mistake bank**: every wrong grammar answer becomes a review card.
- **Generated drills**: der/die/das, adjective endings, 90 strong verbs, prepositions & cases, word order.
- **Speaking & listening**: text-to-speech dictation and speech recognition for shadowing.
- **Placement test**, a searchable grammar & vocabulary library, XP, streaks and daily goals.

Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript: no frameworks, no build step and no server.
It works offline, and progress stays in your browser.
